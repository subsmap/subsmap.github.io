About interactive map of land subsidence in Iran

Interactive map of land subsidence in Iran is developed in a collaboration between Leibniz University Hannover and the GFZ German Research Centre for Geosciences. It provides insights into land subsidence in Iran using InSAR analysis of Sentinel-1 satellite data, highlights regions affected by land subsidence and aids in monitoring and understanding subsidence trends.

The map contains two sets of subsidence products:

  • LUH
    The dataset is derived from a nationwide InSAR analysis of Sentinel-1 data from 2014 to 2020. InSAR Line-of-Sight (LOS) deformations are projected to vertical rates assuming negligible horizontal motions. For details on the analysis, please refer to the original paper published open access in Science Advances. You can also download the data as GeoTiff for further analysis from the Zenodo repository. For questions related to the LUH results, please contact mahmud [at] ipi.uni-hannover.de

  • COMET
    This dataset includes results from an independent study by Payne et al., 2025 processed at the Centre for the Observation and Modelling of Earthquakes, Volcanoes and Tectonics (COMET). The analysis consists of both ascending and descending Sentinel-1 data from 2014 to 2022 and provides vertical and east-west components of displacement. Subsidence rates are derived from the vertical component with a 1-cm/year threshold. Accompanying software for paper and time series data are available at the Zenodo repository and COMET subsidence portal. For questions related to the COMET results, please contact j.elliott [at] leeds.ac.uk

Acknowledgment

This service utilizes various packages and data sources, including:

  • The interactive map is created using Leaflet.
  • GeoServer is used as the Web Map Service.
  • The Vazirmatn font is used for Persian text.
  • leaflet-groupedlayercontrol is utilized for grouping layers.
  • GDAL and QGIS are used for post-processing InSAR results.
  • Scientific colour maps is used for some colorbars.
  • OpenStreetMap® is used in the terrain map.
  • Copernicus DEM - Global and European Digital Elevation Model is used in the terrain map.
  • Land Cover 2020 (raster 10 m), global, annual - version 1 is used in the terrain map.
  • The map contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2014-2020, processed by ESA.
